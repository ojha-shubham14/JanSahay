# JanSahay

### AI-Driven Scheme Matching for Marginalized Entrepreneurs

> A multilingual digital platform that simplifies access to concessional government loan schemes by helping users identify suitable schemes, understand repayment obligations, and find relevant Channel Partners.


## Overview

JanSahay is a digital platform developed to address the difficulty faced by Scheduled Caste beneficiaries in understanding and accessing concessional loan schemes through the Channel Finance System.

Applicants may face challenges in:

- Identifying which loan scheme is suitable for their needs
- Understanding eligibility requirements
- Estimating EMI and total repayment
- Understanding the impact of interest rates and moratorium periods
- Finding a relevant Channel Partner
- Navigating financial information that is often presented in complex terminology
- Accessing information in their preferred regional language

JanSahay brings these requirements together into a single, simple and guided user experience.


## Problem Statement

Under the Channel Finance System, eligible beneficiaries access concessional financial assistance through designated Channel Partners.

However, users may struggle to determine:

- Which scheme is applicable to their requirement
- Whether they meet the relevant eligibility conditions
- How much they would need to repay
- Which Channel Partner is relevant for their loan category
- Where a suitable Channel Partner is located

JanSahay addresses these challenges through an integrated platform consisting of:

1. Smart Scheme Recommender
2. Financial Calculator
3. Geo-Spatial Channel Partner Locator

## Solution

JanSahay provides a guided flow that takes the user's requirements and helps them move from **need → scheme → financial understanding → Channel Partner**.
